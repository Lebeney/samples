# Regulatory Perimeter for an AI Personal-Finance Assistant — Status as of July 26, 2026

All findings below are from live web sources checked 2026-07-26. Labels: **VERIFIED** (multiple/current authoritative sources), **PARTIAL** (directionally confirmed, detail gap), **NOT FOUND**.

---

## 1. United States

### CFPB §1033 open banking — VERIFIED (in flux)
- The Oct-2024 Personal Financial Data Rights rule is **codified but unenforceable**. In the *Forcht Bank v. CFPB* litigation (E.D. Ky.), the CFPB first moved to vacate its own rule (May-June 2025), then reversed course on July 29, 2025, obtaining a stay of litigation to run an "accelerated rulemaking" ([Consumer Financial Services Law Monitor, July 2025](https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2025/07/cfpb-section-1033-open-banking-rule-stayed-as-cfpb-initiates-new-rulemaking/); [MoFo, Aug 26, 2025](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/250826-on-reconsideration-cfpb-issues-another-anpr)).
- An **ANPR ("Personal Financial Data Rights Reconsideration") published Aug 22, 2025** ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/22/2025-16139/personal-financial-data-rights-reconsideration)), comments closed Oct 21, 2025, reopening four issues: who counts as an authorized "representative"/third party, **fees for data access**, data security, and data privacy ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/08/cfpb-seeks-comments-and-data-on-revised-open-banking-rule); [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2025/2025-08-27-cfpb-reconsiders-section-1033-rule-signaling-potential-overhaul-of-personal-financial-data-rights-framework)).
- Judge Danny Reeves **preliminarily enjoined enforcement** (Nov 2025), finding the rule likely exceeded statutory authority ([ABA Banking Journal, Nov 2025](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2025/11/kentucky-federal-court-enjoins-cfpb-from-enforcing-current-1033-final-rule/); [Moore & Van Allen](https://www.mvalaw.com/data-points/cfpb-enjoined-from-enforcing-personal-financial-data-rights-rule-1033)). The **April 1, 2026 first compliance date passed without effect** ([Cozen O'Connor, 2026](https://www.cozen.com/news-resources/publications/2026/section-1033-compliance-date-open-banking-rule-enjoined-and-under-reconsideration)).
- **PARTIAL:** a replacement NPRM/final rule had not verifiably issued by July 2026; the CFPB had said it aimed to finalize "by year-end," and by mid-2026 attention was shifting to **state data-sharing laws** filling the vacuum ([Consumer Finance Monitor, June 26, 2026](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/06/26/open-banking-regulation-in-2026-federal-regulation-resurfaces-as-states-bring-data-sharing-into-focus/); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/cfpb-revamps-1033-open-banking-rule-with-new-focus-on-fees)). Practical consequence for a PFM: aggregator access (Plaid et al.) continues contractually, but a fee-permissive rewrite could raise data-access costs.

### CFPB chatbot guidance & posture — PARTIAL / VERIFIED
- The June 2023 ["Chatbots in consumer finance" Issue Spotlight](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/cfpb-issue-spotlight-analyzes-artificial-intelligence-chatbots-in-banking/) remains posted, but it was a report, not binding guidance. The CFPB **withdrew 67 guidance documents on May 12, 2025** ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/12/2025-08286/interpretive-rules-policy-statements-and-advisory-opinions-withdrawal); [Morgan Lewis tracker](https://www.morganlewis.com/topics/cfpb-guidance-tracker-rescinded-and-remaining)); the chatbot spotlight was not on that list, but its practical force under current leadership is minimal — **PARTIAL**.
- Leadership: Russell Vought (acting) pursued a ~53% staff cut (to ~550 employees, pending court approval) and is expected to depart around Aug 2026; **Brian Johnson nominated as permanent director** ([Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/cfpb-workforce-reduction-plan-vought-cut-53-percent-618-nteu-farman-big-beautiful-fed-funding/816489/); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/cfpb-vought-new-nomination/)). Net: federal UDAAP enforcement risk against a PFM chatbot is low near-term; **FTC and state AGs are the live enforcers** (see §4).

### SEC / FINRA / Advisers Act — VERIFIED
- The SEC **formally withdrew the 2023 predictive-data-analytics conflicts proposal in June 2025** ([SEC S7-12-23](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2025/06/s7-12-23); [Stinson](https://www.stinson.com/newsroom-publications-sec-withdraws-proposed-rules-affecting-investment-advisers-funds-and-broker-dealers)). Chair Atkins takes a principles-based line — at the Dec 4, 2025 Investor Advisory Committee he said existing disclosure rules suffice ([Advisor Perspectives, May 2026](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2026/05/06/sec-isnt-coming-thats-problem)). But **FY2026 exam priorities include accuracy of AI representations and AI supervision policies** ([Cleary](https://www.clearyenforcementwatch.com/2025/11/sec-exam-priorities-2026-priorities-largely-consistent-will-approach-to-deficiencies-and-enforcement-referrals-change/)); "AI-washing" enforcement continues under existing antifraud rules ([InnReg](https://www.innreg.com/blog/sec-guidance-on-ai)).
- FINRA: no new AI rule; **Regulatory Notice 24-09** plus a dedicated GenAI section (governance, testing, hallucination controls, AI-agent risks) in the **2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report (Dec 9, 2025)** ([FINRA](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-finra-annual-regulatory-oversight-report/gen-ai); [Debevoise, Dec 11, 2025](https://www.debevoisedatablog.com/2025/12/11/finras-2026-regulatory-oversight-report-continued-focus-on-generative-ai-and-emerging-agent-based-risks/)).
- **Advisers Act line (unchanged law):** IAA registration triggers on providing *advice about securities*, for compensation, as a business — personalization is the key aggravator; budgeting/spending/savings guidance not touching securities stays outside, while personalized "invest more in X" outputs cross the line ([Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/artificial-intelligence-compliance-considerations-investment-advisers-sec-securities-exchange-commission-legal-regulation-framework/)). Notably, **Origin launched an "SEC-regulated AI financial advisor" (Sept 9, 2025) through Origin Investment Advisory LLC, an SEC-registered RIA** — the first firm to register the AI channel rather than rely on educational framing ([Business Wire, Sept 9, 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250909759834/en/Origin-Unveils-First-AI-Financial-Advisor-Regulated-by-the-SEC-Outsmarts-Every-Leading-AI-Model-on-the-CFP-Exam)).

### GLBA + state privacy — VERIFIED
~20 state comprehensive privacy laws are in effect/pending in 2026, and states are **narrowing GLBA exemptions from entity-level to data-level** (Montana, Connecticut; Minnesota-style amendments effective July 1, 2026 pull non-depository fintechs in) ([Orrick, July 2025](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2025/07/Where-is-the-GLBA-Entity-Level-Exemption-Two-More-State-Privacy-Laws); [Axiom](https://www.axiomlaw.com/blog/state-privacy-laws)). A House Financial Services **GLBA-amendment/state-preemption discussion draft** circulated March 2026 ([CFS Law Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2026/03/house-financial-services-committee-discussion-draft-to-amend-glba-and-preempt-state-regimes/)) — not law. A PFM must run GLBA Safeguards/privacy-notice compliance **plus** state-law rights (access/deletion/profiling opt-outs) for non-GLBA data.

## 2. EU / UK

### PSD3/PSR — VERIFIED
Provisional political agreement **Nov 27, 2025**; texts published Apr 23, 2026; COREPER endorsed Apr 22, 2026, ECON vote May 5, 2026, plenary May 2026; **Official Journal publication expected ~June-Sept 2026, application generally 21 months later (~2028)** ([MoFo, Apr 30, 2026](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260430-psd3-and-psr-key-developments); [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/cedd39c6/psd3-and-psr-from-provisional-agreement-to-2026-readiness)). AIS (account information services — the PFM's license category) moves largely into the directly applicable PSR with a lighter registration regime.

### FiDA — VERIFIED (still not adopted)
FiDA survived the early-2025 withdrawal scare (it appeared on the Commission's 2025 work-programme discussion lists) and is **included in the 2026 Work Programme**; first trilogue April 2025; **still in trilogue as of spring 2026**, with open fights over gatekeeper/big-tech access and scope; realistic adoption 2026-27, phased application ~2028-2030 via Financial Data Sharing Schemes ([Konsentus](https://www.konsentus.com/fidas-timetable-might-shift-but-the-destination-wont/); [Bankenverband](https://bankenverband.de/en/digital-world/trilogue-negotiations-framework-financial-data-access-fida-regulation); [KPMG Cyprus, Feb 2026](https://kpmg.com/cy/en/home/insights/2026/02/introduction-to-fida-understanding-the-financial-data-access-regulation.html)). Until then, PFM data access in the EU rests on PSD2 AIS licensing + GDPR.

### EU AI Act — VERIFIED (major 2026 change)
- **Digital Omnibus provisional agreement postpones Annex III high-risk obligations from Aug 2, 2026 to Dec 2, 2027** (Annex I embedded systems to Aug 2, 2028), with formal adoption expected before Aug 2, 2026 ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/); [Covington/Inside Global Tech, May 28, 2026](https://www.insideglobaltech.com/2026/05/28/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/)).
- Classification: a PFM assistant is **not per-se high-risk**; it is a consumer-facing AI system subject to **Article 50 transparency** (users must know they're talking to AI; AI-generated content disclosure), which **still applies Aug 2, 2026** despite the omnibus ([Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html)). **Creditworthiness assessment/credit scoring is Annex III high-risk** — if the PFM scores users for credit or feeds lending decisions, the high-risk regime attaches (now from Dec 2027).
- GPAI: obligations live since **Aug 2, 2025** (Code of Practice published July 10, 2025); **Commission/AI Office enforcement powers and fines from Aug 2, 2026**; legacy models to comply by Aug 2, 2027 ([Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-gpai-model-obligations-in-force-and-final-gpai-code-of-practice-in-place); [MediaLaws](https://www.medialaws.eu/eu-ai-obligations-for-gpai-providers-compliance-enforcement-deadlines-2025-2027/)). A PFM building on a third-party model is a deployer, not GPAI provider (unless it fine-tunes at scale).

### UK — VERIFIED
- **No AI-specific FCA rules**: tech-neutral, outcomes-based reliance on **Consumer Duty + SM&CR**, confirmed to Parliament and in the FCA's growth-letter response; AI Lab running, **AI Live Testing cohort 2 applications Jan 19-Mar 24, 2026**, evaluation report due Q1 2027 ([FCA AI Lab](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/ai-lab); [FCA blog](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/blogs/ai-financial-services-approach); [Covington, Apr 9, 2026](https://www.insideglobaltech.com/2026/04/09/uk-financial-services-regulators-approach-to-artificial-intelligence-in-2026/)). Consumer Duty implication: firm is accountable for AI-generated guidance outcomes (fair value, consumer understanding, foreseeable harm) regardless of disclaimers.
- **Targeted support is now real**: policy statement with near-final rules Dec 11, 2025; **Board made rules final Feb 26, 2026 (FCA 2026/5); regime effective Apr 6, 2026, authorisation gateway open since March 2026** — firms can give group-based "people like you" suggestions on investments and pensions without full advice ([FCA AGBR page](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/advice-guidance-boundary-review); [FCA instrument 2026/5](https://api-handbook.fca.org.uk/files/instrument/GLOSSARY-SYSC-TC-FEES-MIFIDPRU-IPRUINV-COBS-ICOBS-FPCOB-PDCOB-PROD-SUP-DISP-COLL-CREDS-FUND-PERG/FCA%202026/5-2026-03-02.pdf); [Freshfields](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/risk-and-compliance/fca-finalises-targeted-support-rules-applications-now-open-102mmq0)). A further consultation on simplified advice / the information-guidance boundary was slated for 2026. This is the most PFM-favorable advice-boundary development anywhere — but it requires FCA authorisation with the targeted-support permission.

## 3. Nigeria

### CBN Open Banking — PARTIAL
CBN announced (Apr 29, 2025) an **August 2025 launch**; the commercial launch **slipped**, and a **phased go-live is now rolling out through 2026** per CBN's February 2026 policy commitments, with NIBSS running the Open Banking Registry and Consent Management System (OBCMS); adoption remains early, with sandbox participation by Sterling/Sparkle, Wema/ALAT, FCMB ([Open Banking Nigeria](https://openbanking.ng/open-banking-in-nigeria/); [MobiBank, 2026](https://www.mobibank.fi/nigeria-open-banking-africas-first-rail-goes-live-in-2026/); [CBN Operational Guidelines 2023](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2023/CCD/Operational%20Guidelines%20for%20Open%20Banking%20in%20Nigeria.pdf)). Exact live-participant counts as of July 2026: **NOT FOUND**. A PFM needs API Consumer status via the OBR, tiered by data sensitivity, with explicit purpose/duration-bound consent.

### NDPA 2023 + GAID 2025 — VERIFIED
The **GAID (issued Mar 20, 2025, effective Sept 19, 2025)** replaced the NDPR 2019 and operationalizes the NDPA: registration of controllers "of major importance" (most fintechs qualify), DPO designation, annual compliance audit returns, breach notification, cross-border transfer rules (adequacy/safeguards), and extraterritorial reach over foreign entities targeting Nigerian data subjects ([IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/from-principles-to-practice-operationalizing-nigerias-data-protection-act-through-the-gaid); [Banwo & Ighodalo](https://www.banwo-ighodalo.com/grey-matter/synoptic-analysis-of-the-nigeria-data-protection-act-general-application-and-implementation-directive-gaid-2025/); [Templars](https://www.templars-law.com/app/uploads/2025/04/NDPC-Issues-the-Nigeria-Data-Protection-Act-General-Application-and-Implementation-Directive-2025.pdf)). **Data residency:** no blanket localization in the NDPA/GAID (transfers permitted with adequacy or safeguards), but **CBN guidelines require banking/payment data processed under the open banking framework to be stored per CBN/NDPR security requirements**, and sector rules effectively favor in-country hosting for core payment data — **PARTIAL** on hard-localization scope.

## 4. Cross-cutting: disclaimers and the "educational, not advice" line

- **Cleo** (terms): "Cleo does not intend to provide you with any legal, tax, or financial advice… Cleo is not a lawyer, tax advisor, broker, or financial planner" ([Cleo T&Cs](https://web.meetcleo.com/page/term-conditions)) — VERIFIED.
- **Intuit**: services "are not and shall not be deemed or construed to be legal, financial or investment advice"; "Intuit does not give professional advice" ([Intuit website ToS](https://www.intuit.com/legal/terms/en-us/website/); [Mint ToS](https://mint.intuit.com/terms)) — VERIFIED (generic terms; Intuit-Assist-specific disclosure text: PARTIAL).
- **Origin**: went the opposite way — registered the AI channel under **Origin Investment Advisory LLC (SEC RIA)**, with disclosure that the AI gives "personalized, non-discretionary financial guidance… may occasionally contain errors," users responsible for decisions ([Business Wire, Sept 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250909759834/en/Origin-Unveils-First-AI-Financial-Advisor-Regulated-by-the-SEC-Outsmarts-Every-Leading-AI-Model-on-the-CFP-Exam)) — VERIFIED.
- **Copilot Money** disclaimer text: **NOT FOUND** in accessible sources.
- **Does the framing hold up?** Disclaimers do not immunize conduct: the **FTC's $17M settlement with Cleo AI (Mar 27, 2025)** shows AI-branded PFM apps are being policed for deceptive claims (advance amounts/speed, cancellation friction) irrespective of advice disclaimers ([FTC case page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/cleo-ai-inc-ftc-v); [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/ftc-reaches-17-million-settlement-with-cash-advance-company-cleo-ai)). SEC exams test whether AI marketing matches reality ("AI-washing"); under the Advisers Act and UK FSMA, **substance over label** governs — personalized securities/investment recommendations are advice no matter the disclaimer, while genuinely generic budgeting content is safe. In the UK, the new targeted-support regime plus Consumer Duty means "educational" framing is weakest there: outcomes, not labels, are judged.

## Disclosure-language notes (draft)

1. **AI identity (mandatory EU from Aug 2, 2026; best practice everywhere):** "You're chatting with an AI assistant, not a human. It can make mistakes — verify important information."
2. **Advice perimeter:** "Content is for general informational and educational purposes only and is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice; [Company] is not acting as your fiduciary, adviser, or broker. Consider consulting a licensed professional." Keep product behavior consistent with this: no individualized security-specific recommendations, or register (US RIA / UK targeted-support permission) as Origin did.
3. **Accuracy/hallucination:** disclose that outputs are generated from your linked data and may be incomplete or wrong; provide an error-reporting path (FINRA/FCA governance expectations).
4. **Marketing discipline:** every AI capability claim must be substantiated (FTC v. Cleo; SEC AI-washing exams) — the disclaimer will not save overstated marketing.
5. **Data:** layered GLBA notice + state-privacy rights (US); GDPR/PSD2 consent (EU); NDPA/GAID purpose-and-duration-bound consent language (Nigeria).
