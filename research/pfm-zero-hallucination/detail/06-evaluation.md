# Evaluation Methodology for an NL-to-Analytics Personal-Finance Assistant (as of 2026-07-26)

All sources accessed live 2026-07-26. Snowflake's own blog pages returned 403 to fetches, so those specifics rest on search-indexed excerpts — labeled accordingly.

## 1. Financial QA benchmarks — and fact-check of D4

**VERIFIED — the established benchmarks are all document-QA over filings/reports, not transaction analytics.**

- **FinQA / ConvFinQA**: multi-step arithmetic over earnings-report excerpts (text + tables), with gold reasoning programs; ConvFinQA adds multi-turn conversational state over the same documents ([ConvFinQA paper listing](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372928237_ConvFinQA_Exploring_the_Chain_of_Numerical_Reasoning_in_Conversational_Finance_Question_Answering), orig. 2022).
- **TAT-QA**: 16,552 questions over 2,757 hybrid table+text financial-report contexts; span extraction, counting, arithmetic (per surveys, e.g. [FinanceComplexQA, arXiv 2607.19238](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19238v1), 2026).
- **DocMath-Eval**: numerical reasoning over long specialized documents, four difficulty tiers (short/long doc × simple/complex reasoning) ([docmath-eval.github.io](https://docmath-eval.github.io/); [arXiv 2311.09805](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09805), 2023).
- **BizBench**: 8 quantitative-reasoning tasks over structured/unstructured financial data via program synthesis (code generation), incl. SEC-filing extraction and financial formulas ([arXiv 2311.06602](https://axi.lims.ac.uk/paper/2311.06602), 2023).
- **Newer (2024–2026)**: **FinBen** — 42 datasets / 24 financial NLP tasks, still corporate-document and market oriented. **FailSafeQA** ([arXiv 2502.06329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06329), Feb 2025) — long-context finance QA *robustness* (perturbed queries, degraded/irrelevant/empty documents), scored by LLM-as-judge on Robustness/Grounding/Compliance — useful as a design pattern for perturbation testing, not as a dataset. 2025–2026 additions (Finance Agent Benchmark [arXiv 2508.00828]; FinTrace tool-calling trajectories [arXiv 2604.10015]; BizFinBench.v2 [arXiv 2601.06401]) evaluate research-analyst and tool-calling workflows over filings/market data.
- **Transaction-analytics-specific benchmark: NOT FOUND.** The closest artifacts are a large-scale anonymized bank transaction/geo/support-chat release (Mollaev et al., 2025, cited in the [CLEF-2026 FinMMEval lab overview](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.10886)) and BankMathBench (banking numerical reasoning, [arXiv 2602.17072]) — neither is a personal-spending NL-analytics benchmark.

**Verdict on D4: VERIFIED.** Reuse their *question taxonomies* (multi-step arithmetic, conversational follow-ups, perturbation robustness), not their data.

## 2. Published NL-to-analytics eval methodology

- **Databricks AI/BI Genie** (**VERIFIED**, [docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/benchmarks](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/genie/benchmarks) and [learn.microsoft.com Genie benchmarks](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/databricks/genie/benchmarks)): up to **500 benchmark questions per space**; each question carries a gold SQL query (or UC SQL function) and correctness is judged by **comparing result sets**, not SQL strings. Best practices: cover the most-asked questions, include **2–4 phrasings per question**, run benchmarks on every space refinement, and treat benchmark failures as the metadata/instructions to-do list ([Databricks blog: building high-performing Genie spaces](https://databricks.com/blog/data-dialogue-best-practices-guide-building-high-performing-genie-spaces)).
- **Snowflake Cortex Analyst** (**PARTIAL** — 403 on direct fetch; numbers from indexed Snowflake pages): ~**90% accuracy** "reliably across customer evaluations and benchmark tests" on an internal ~**150-question** eval set; ~2x single-shot LLM SQL and ~14% above a named competitor ([Snowflake blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/cortex-analyst-ai-self-service-analytics/), 2024; [Mistral customer story](https://mistral.ai/customers/snowflake/)). Methodologically, Snowflake's **Verified Query Repository** doubles as golden set and few-shot store, and [Cortex Analyst evaluations](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-analyst-evaluations) test semantic views against their own verified queries as ground truth (**VERIFIED** docs).
- **Golden-set construction guidance** (**PARTIAL**, blog-grade): 100–300 cases as a defensible enterprise starting point with roughly **60% common / 30% tricky / 10% adversarial** mix ([trustevals.ai](https://www.trustevals.ai/resources/golden-datasets-for-ai-evaluation)); result-set comparison pitfalls — aliases, ordering, redundant columns ([Tiger Data text-to-SQL eval](https://www.tigerdata.com/blog/text-to-sql-eval-open-source)).
- **Context for targets**: BIRD execution accuracy ~73–82% for top pipelines vs ~93% human; Spider 2.0 (enterprise-realistic, ~800-column schemas) ~21–30% even agentically ([BIRD gap analysis](https://beancount.io/bean-labs/research-logs/2026/06/06/bird-benchmark-text-to-sql-real-database-gap), Jun 2026). A constrained QuerySpec over one known schema should far exceed open text-to-SQL numbers.

## 3. Synthetic transaction data generators

- **PaySim** (**VERIFIED**, [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ealaxi/paysim1)): ~6M simulated *mobile-money* transactions, fraud-oriented. **Poor PFM realism** — no merchants/categories/household patterns.
- **IBM TabFormer** (**VERIFIED**, [github.com/IBM/TabFormer](https://github.com/IBM/TabFormer), 2021): 24M synthetic credit-card transactions, 12 fields incl. merchant/MCC. Best public volume, but fraud-modeling oriented; category semantics thin for PFM QA.
- **IBM AMLSim** (**VERIFIED**, [wiki](https://github.com/IBM/AMLSim/wiki/Program:-Transaction-Simulator)): AML typology simulator — wrong domain.
- **J.P. Morgan AI Research synthetic data** (**VERIFIED**, [jpmorganchase.com synthetic data page](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/about/technology/research/ai/synthetic-data)): public non-commercial payments/fraud, AML, customer-journey datasets — institutional, not household PFM.
- **Plaid Sandbox** (**VERIFIED**, [docs](https://plaid.com/docs/sandbox/user-custom/), [plaid/sandbox-custom-users](https://github.com/plaid/sandbox-custom-users)): `user_custom` JSON personas, `user_transactions_dynamic` with realistic history, `/sandbox/transactions/create` for injecting transactions. **Best schema realism** (real Plaid fields incl. personal-finance categories) but limited behavioral depth/volume.
- **Assessment**: nothing public gives you a realistic, ground-truth-labeled year of household spending. The pragmatic path is a **deterministic generator emitting Plaid-schema transactions** (recurring bills, salary, seasonal noise, refunds, transfers, duplicates) where ground-truth answers are computable by construction.

## 4. Eval tooling (mid-2026)

- **Promptfoo**: **acquisition by OpenAI announced 2026-03-09** ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-promptfoo/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/09/openai-acquires-promptfoo-to-secure-its-ai-agents/)) — **VERIFIED**. Remains open source, but roadmap is tilting to red-teaming/OpenAI Frontier; a risk for a long-lived regression harness.
- **DeepEval** (**PARTIAL** on pricing): pytest-style, free OSS, trivially blocks PRs on assertion thresholds; Confident AI cloud from ~$19.99/user/mo ([Confident AI comparison](https://www.confident-ai.com/knowledge-base/compare/top-braintrust-alternatives-and-competitors-compared)).
- **Langfuse** (**VERIFIED** via docs): OSS, easiest full Docker self-host; **datasets + experiments + code evaluators** explicitly supporting deterministic exact-match/custom-rule scores ([code evaluators](https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/evaluation-methods/code-evaluators)), plus an official **GitHub Action that runs an experiment against a dataset, comments on the PR, and fails the job on regression** ([experiments in CI/CD](https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/experiments/experiments-ci-cd); [langfuse/experiment-action](https://github.com/langfuse/experiment-action)).
- **Braintrust** (**PARTIAL**): strongest enterprise self-hosted option tied to release control; seat + volume SaaS pricing ([Braintrust self-hosted comparison](https://www.braintrust.dev/articles/best-self-hosted-ai-evals-tools-2026)).
- **Best fit for NL→QuerySpec→number**: **Langfuse (self-hosted) for dataset versioning, tracing, and CI gating, with plain pytest/DeepEval-style deterministic assertions as the scorer layer.** Your scorers are exact/tolerance numeric matches — no LLM-judge needed — so avoid paying for judge-centric platforms.

## 5. Published numeric-accuracy targets/SLOs

- **Snowflake Cortex Analyst: ~90%** on internal ~150-question sets (**PARTIAL** — vendor-internal, not independently verified).
- **ThoughtSpot Sage docs: ~60% accuracy on complex models** (thousands of columns, overlapping names), with explicit "always review the answer" guidance ([docs.thoughtspot.com Sage best practices](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/10.14.0.cl/sage-search-best-practices)) — **VERIFIED**, and a usefully honest floor.
- **Databricks Genie**: publishes methodology, **no public accuracy SLO — NOT FOUND**.
- No industry-standard numeric-correctness SLO exists; ~90% on a curated internal set is the de facto "enterprise-credible" bar, and vendors uniformly hedge with human-review guidance.

## Recommended eval plan skeleton

1. **Golden set: 250–300 items** (Databricks-style, well under their 500/space ceiling; above the 100–300 blog floor). Construction: (a) enumerate your QuerySpec grammar (aggregate × groupby × time-window × category/merchant filter × comparison) and cover every operator at least 5×; (b) 60% expected user questions, 30% tricky (ambiguous dates, "last month" vs "past 30 days", refunds/transfers exclusion, pending transactions), 10% adversarial/out-of-scope (FailSafeQA-style perturbations: typos, vague or unanswerable questions — expected answer = clarify/refuse); (c) **2–4 phrasings per canonical question** counted as separate cases.
2. **Fixture data**: deterministic synthetic generator emitting Plaid-schema transactions (3–5 personas × 12–24 months); ground-truth numbers computed by an independent reference implementation, never by the model.
3. **Metrics**: (i) QuerySpec exact match (structural); (ii) **executed-result numeric match** (exact for counts/sums at cent precision; result-set comparison for group-bys, order-insensitive); (iii) correct-refusal rate on out-of-scope; (iv) latency. Track (ii) as the headline number.
4. **CI gating**: run full set on every PR touching prompts/schema/model via Langfuse `experiment-action`; **fail below 90% numeric match overall and below 100% on a ~50-item "never-break" core subset**; no per-case regressions on core.
5. **Tooling**: self-hosted Langfuse (datasets, experiment diffs, tracing) + pytest deterministic scorers; revisit Braintrust only if you need managed enterprise features. Log production questions and fold weekly misses back into the golden set, Genie-style ("benchmark failures are the to-do list").
