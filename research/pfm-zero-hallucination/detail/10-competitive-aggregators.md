# AI Personal-Finance Assistants & Bank-Data Aggregators — Research Teardown (as of 2026-07-26)

All findings from live web searches, 2026-07-26. Labels: **VERIFIED** (primary/official source), **PARTIAL** (secondary source or incomplete detail), **NOT FOUND**.

## Part A — How shipping products ground AI answers over transactions

### 1. Intuit GenOS / Intuit Assist — VERIFIED
Intuit has published the most detailed architecture of any incumbent. Key sources: the Intuit Engineering Medium post ["How to Accelerate Development Velocity in the GenAI Era? Build a GenOS"](https://medium.com/intuit-engineering/how-to-accelerate-development-velocity-in-the-genai-era-build-a-genos-e71ac2e17b82) and the [Intuit blog on GenOS enhancements](https://www.intuit.com/blog/innovative-thinking/accelerating-development-velocity-with-major-enhancements-to-genos/) (2024).
- **GenRuntime** is the serving layer: a **GenOrchestrator** with a *Planner* (intent → agent/tool plan), *Executor*, and *Memory + Knowledge Retrieval* to "ensure factual correctness." Grounding is via RAG plus a plugin mechanism of agents/tools over Intuit domain data — i.e., **numbers come from deterministic tool calls, the LLM does planning and phrasing**.
- **GenSRF** (security, risk, fraud) provides guardrails for GenAI deployments; the internal [Custom LLM Leaderboard](https://medium.com/intuit-engineering/intuits-custom-llm-leaderboard-optimizing-model-selection-for-financial-use-cases-ac08d467f8f3) (Oct 2024) benchmarks models on accuracy, latency, cost, security, safety; Intuit's [AI research program post](https://medium.com/intuit-engineering/intuits-ai-research-program-producing-concrete-results-for-applied-ai-at-scale-with-llms-9be755cac5b0) describes in-house hallucination-detection and uncertainty metrics wired into eval. A dedicated public post on prompt-injection defense specifically: **NOT FOUND** (GenSRF is mentioned only at a high level).

### 2. PFM apps
- **Cleo — VERIFIED, best-documented startup.** Cleo's engineering blog is explicit that **"for precise tasks like filtering transactions and performing calculations, Cleo uses deterministic tools to do the actual math, while the LLM layer interprets user requests and generates responses"** — see [Building a financial agent on top of commodified LLMs](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/building-a-financial-agent-on-top-of-commodified-llms), [Introducing Cleo 3.0](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/introducing-cleo-3-0) (agentic tool selection, long-term memory, trust safeguards), [How Cleo learns what your transactions actually mean](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/how-cleo-learns-what-your-transactions-actually-mean) (per-transaction real-time classification, cheap enough to run on every transaction), [A Year of LLM Developments at Cleo](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/a-year-of-llm-developments-at-cleo) (Feb 2024: FAQ-grounded prompts cut support contacts 9%), and [an eval post comparing Cleo vs generalist models](https://web.meetcleo.com/blog/cleo-vs-the-rest-evaluating-ai-models-on-real-world-money-questions). This is the same pattern as Intuit: LLM = intent + narration; math/filtering = tested domain tools.
- **Monarch Money — PARTIAL.** No engineering blog. Its [help center](https://help.monarch.com/hc/en-us/articles/37526856682260-AI-in-Monarch) confirms "third-party LLMs plus in-house modeling" for the assistant, insights, receipt scanning, categorization. AI job postings (2025–26) reveal internals: multi-agent systems, RAG, eval datasets/scorers, an AI Platform team owning LLM routing, observability, cost attribution.
- **Copilot Money — PARTIAL.** No LLM-assistant architecture published; "Copilot Intelligence" is a **per-user ML categorization model** (features: description, amount, day-of-week, card) per its [help center](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/8182433-copilot-intelligence-for-spending); ~95% first-pass accuracy after corrections claimed in reviews. States data isn't used to train external models.
- **Rocket Money — VERIFIED (enrichment only).** The [Rocket Money × Hugging Face case study](https://huggingface.co/blog/rocketmoney-case-study) documents the evolution regex/decision-table normalizers → bag-of-words model-per-class → scaled transformer classification on Hugging Face infra for merchant/subscription detection. Nothing published on an LLM chat assistant.
- **Emma — PARTIAL.** UK app, 50+ bank connections; markets AI features but no architecture writeups found beyond app-store/marketing copy.
- **Origin — VERIFIED.** The most detailed post-2025 disclosure: [technical overview of its "SEC-regulated AI Financial Advisor"](https://useorigin.com/resources/blog/technical-overview) (Sept 2025, plus [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250909759834/en/)): heterogeneous multi-LLM ensemble (Claude 4.1 Opus, GPT, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Perplexity Sonar), orchestration layer routing to specialized agents, **200+ MCP tools behind an Anthropic-partnered abstraction layer**, 138-point automated compliance/eval checks validating numerical accuracy, cross-model LLM-as-judge verification, real-time grounding, structured memory. Claims CFP-exam outperformance.
- **Quicken Simplifi — PARTIAL.** Shipped "Quicken Assist Chat" ([help article](https://support.simplifi.quicken.com/en/articles/13343650-using-quicken-assist-chat), [community thread](https://community.simplifimoney.com/discussion/15724/new-feature-alert-quicken-assist-chat)): reads user data, proposes in-product actions with explicit user approval before execution. No architecture details.

### 3. Neobanks
- **Nubank — VERIFIED.** Post-Hyperplane (acquired July 2024; [announcement](https://international.nubank.com.br/company/nubank-acquires-hyperplane-to-accelerate-ai-first-strategy/)), Nubank published [Building Foundation Models into Nubank's AI Platform](https://building.nubank.com/foundation-models-ai-nubank-transformation/) and [a foundation-models-for-personalization post](https://building.nubank.com/unlocking-financial-insights-how-nubank-powers-personalized-experiences-with-foundation-models/): billion-parameter transaction-sequence foundation models ("nuFormer") deployed to production decisioning for 100M+ customers; ~1.20% avg AUC lift across credit/lending/income-prediction/cross-sell over 8 months. Note: this is **predictive foundation modeling, not a chat assistant**.
- **Revolut — VERIFIED (launch), PARTIAL (architecture).** Announced an AI assistant in its [2025 vision](https://www.revolut.com/en-HR/news/revolut_reveals_2025_vision_with_ai_assistant_mortgages_and_atms_on_the_horizon/); shipped **AIR (AI by Revolut)** to 13M UK customers starting 9 April 2026 ([Revolut newsroom](https://www.revolut.com/news/revolut_enters_new_era_of_money_intelligence_with_launch_of_ai_assistant/), [FinTech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/news/revolut-air-ai-assistant-uk-customers-launch-2026)). Spending analysis, subscription management, card freezing; zero-data-retention with third-party AI partners. Model/stack undisclosed.
- **Monzo — VERIFIED.** [Machine Learning at Monzo in 2025](https://monzo.com/blog/machine-learning-at-monzo-in-2025): LLM agent for customer operations with "rigorous evaluation systems," fraud, personalization; governance emphasized. No customer-facing money-Q&A assistant documented.
- **Wise — NOT FOUND** for AI-assistant architecture; [Wise Tech Stack 2025](https://medium.com/wise-engineering/wise-tech-stack-2025-update-d0e63fe718c7) covers general engineering; job posts confirm RAG/LLM work internally.
- **Kuda — PARTIAL.** No shipped assistant; announced 2025 AI strategy (customer-service chatbot trained on Nigerian interaction patterns, fraud monitoring, credit scoring) after ₦14.3T Q1-2025 volume ([Businessday](https://businessday.ng/companies/article/kuda-plans-ai-integrations-as-transactions-hit-n14-3tn-in-q1/)).

### 4. Incidents — VERIFIED
- **TurboTax/Intuit Assist + H&R Block (March 2024):** Washington Post's Geoffrey Fowler found Intuit Assist wrong/irrelevant on >half of tax questions; still unhelpful on ~25% after an update ([WaPo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2024/03/04/ai-taxes-turbotax-hrblock-chatbot/), [AIAAIC entry](https://www.aiaaic.org/aiaaic-repository/ai-algorithmic-and-automation-incidents/turbotax-hr-block-chatbots-provide-inaccurate-tax-advice), [Accounting Today](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/intuit-h-r-block-tax-ais-critiqued-on-accuracy-of-answers-many-inaccurate)). The canonical public incident for this space.
- **Cleo AI FTC settlement (Mar 27, 2025):** $17M over deceptive cash-advance amounts/speed and cancellation friction ([FTC case page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/cleo-ai-inc-ftc-v)). Product-claims incident, not an LLM-hallucination incident, but reputationally linked.
- A specific documented postmortem of a PFM assistant reporting **wrong balances/figures**: **NOT FOUND** — only generic coverage (e.g., UPenn study: ChatGPT wrong on financial numbers ~35% of the time, per [Siebert](https://blog.siebert.com/ai-personal-finance-risks)). Takeaway: shipped products avoid this class of error architecturally (deterministic math tools), and the failures that made news were advice-quality, not arithmetic.

### Africa
- **Okra: VERIFIED shut down May 2025** (announced July 2025), returning ~$4–5.5M to investors; cited regulatory lag (open banking not yet enforced) and naira-driven dollar cloud costs; assets/talent to UK's Kernel ([TechCabal/Nairametrics](https://nairametrics.com/2025/07/11/inside-the-fall-of-okra-how-africas-most-promising-open-banking-startup-shut-down-at-its-peak/)).
- **Mono: VERIFIED, acquired by Flutterwave Jan 2026** (first YC-to-YC African exit); operates independently, 3 African countries, launching treasury product ([Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/insight/mono-treasury-management-platform/)). The de facto surviving Nigerian data aggregator.
- **Stitch: VERIFIED but pivoted to payments** — $55M Series B (Apr 2025, QED), acquired Exipay for in-person payments; data aggregation no longer the core ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/04/15/south-africas-payments-startup-stitch-raises-55-million-series-b-funding/)).
- **OnePipe: VERIFIED alive but pivoted** to embedded finance/aviation payments (65% of Nigerian domestic airline payments; $250M processed 2025) — not a bank-data API anymore.
- **Nigeria open banking: PARTIAL/live-ish.** CBN approved launch for Aug 1 2025 ([TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2025/04/29/cbn-launches-open-banking/)); that date **slipped**; CBN's Feb 2026 FinTech report commits to a phased rollout through mid-2026 via NIBSS-run registry, BVN-anchored consent ([Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/insight/delayed-open-banking-launch/)). As of July 2026: rolling out, not fully commercially live.

## Aggregator shortlist recommendation
1. **Plaid (primary, US/CA):** best coverage + the exact primitives an AI assistant needs (Enrich, recurring-stream detection, 24-month `days_requested` sync via `/transactions/sync`). Budget for institution-level gaps and re-auth handling.
2. **MX (enrichment quality) or Finicity (if lending/verification matters)** as secondary/fallback US provider; MX's Extended Transaction History matches Plaid's 24-month depth.
3. **Europe/UK:** Tink or TrueLayer for scale; **Salt Edge** if you want aggregation + enrichment from one mid-market vendor across many geographies. **Avoid GoCardless BAD** (closed to new customers).
4. **Nigeria/Africa:** **Mono** is effectively the only production-grade choice post-Okra; design for the CBN/NIBSS standardized API landing through 2026 as a future second rail.
5. **Cost-sensitive US prototyping:** Teller (100 free live connections, direct APIs) — validate coverage for your users' actual banks first.

Cross-cutting Part A lesson for architecture: every credible shipped assistant (Intuit GenRuntime, Cleo, Origin) converges on the same split — **deterministic tools compute every number; the LLM only plans, routes, and narrates**, wrapped in eval harnesses (Intuit's leaderboard/GenSRF, Origin's 138-point checks, Cleo's model evals). The one major public failure (Intuit Assist, WaPo 2024) was in free-form advice, the layer without deterministic grounding.
